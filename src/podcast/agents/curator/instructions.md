# Listener Taste Profile

- **Lead Story (`#1` — headliner):** The front-page story everyone is talking about — major frontier AI launch, big tech/business milestone, or high-impact public/policy shift.
- **Topic priority (highest to lowest):**
  1. **Frontier AI & major tech industry news:** flagship model releases, lab milestones, AI science breakthroughs.
  2. **Tech, business, society & policy:** market shifts, enterprise adoption, regulation, education/workplace studies, cultural impact.
  3. **Software engineering & AI dev:** engineering essays, coding agents, verification, developer workflows.
  4. **Security & privacy:** encryption/policy battles, major telemetry or vulnerability news.
  5. **Hardware & systems:** broad market or architectural shifts only.
- **Skip unless major mainstream news:** Show HN, hobby/retro projects, niche kernel/compiler internals, and crypto/Web3.
